1. Vocabulary – 词汇

Learn the Chinese characters, Pinyin and meanings. Listen and repeat.

2. Grammar – 语法

Pattern 1: Asking someone's name

你叫什么名字？
Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
What is your name?
我叫安。
Wǒ jiào Ān.
My name is An.

Pattern 2: Asking about nationality

你是哪国人？
Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?
What is your nationality?
我是越南人。
Wǒ shì Yuènán rén.
I am Vietnamese.

Pattern 3: The verb 是 (shì)

Structure: Subject + 是 + noun

我是学生。 – I am a student.
他是老师。 – He is a teacher.

Pattern 4: The question particle 吗

Put 吗 at the end of a statement to make a yes/no question.

你是越南人吗？ – Are you Vietnamese?

3. Pronunciation – 发音

Learn Mandarin tones, Pinyin and natural sentence rhythm.

A. The four tones and the neutral tone

Mandarin uses four main tones and one neutral tone. Tone changes the meaning of a syllable.

1st tone – mā

High and level. Keep your voice high and steady.

2nd tone – má

Rising. Your voice moves upwards.

3rd tone – mǎ

Low dipping tone. The full third tone falls and rises.

4th tone – mà

Sharp falling tone. Start high and fall quickly.

Neutral tone – ma

Short and light. Do not stress it.

Note: The audio examples are individual characters. Their tones may sound different in connected speech.
